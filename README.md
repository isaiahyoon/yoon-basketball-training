# YBT — Yoon Basketball Training

**Sharpen your skills. Grow with patience. Play with joy.**

YBT is a personal basketball coaching and training project. This site introduces the training philosophy and coaching services.

YBT is planned as a sole proprietorship.

## Training philosophy

- Build a strong foundation in shooting, dribbling, and other basketball fundamentals.
- Develop game feel, basketball IQ, and decision-making.
- Train with game-like pressure to build composure and mental toughness.
- Practice patiently and consistently with drills tailored to each player.
- Grow as a balanced player on both offense and defense.
- Develop teamwork and communication, learning to make teammates better.
- Make development enjoyable.

## Sessions

- **Who:** Players of all ages who want to improve their game.
- **Schedule and price:** Once a week, for one hour per session at $55. Any time beyond the hour is not charged.
- **Location:** Pan Pacific Park in Los Angeles is the intended location, subject to authorization for paid coaching. Piedmont Park in Atlanta is a possible future location if Isaiah moves there.
- **Payment:** Payment is due within one hour after the session ends. If payment is not received by then, training will stop. Payment methods are Cash App or Venmo (both @pyrolazuli), Zelle (323-774-8524), or cash.
- **Cancellation:** At least 24 hours' notice incurs no charge. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice and no-shows incur a $22 fee, with discretion to waive it for emergencies.
- **Weather:** Sessions affected by rain or unsafe court conditions can be rescheduled at no charge.
- **Coach cancellation:** If Isaiah cancels, there is no charge and the session can be rescheduled.

Park authorization and minor-safety policies remain to be confirmed.

## Website

The site is static. Serve the repository root with any static file server, for example `python3 -m http.server 8000`, and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

English is the default; Spanish and Korean are available through `?lang=es` and `?lang=ko`. The language selector retains other query parameters and the current page fragment.

The social preview uses the approved logo. Once the public site URL is known, set the Open Graph and Twitter image URLs in `index.html` to the absolute URL of `assets/social-preview.png` so social crawlers can reliably fetch it.
