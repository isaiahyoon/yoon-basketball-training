details.

Clear expectations make more room for the good stuff.

How does payment work?

Payment is due within one hour after the session ends. If payment isn’t received within that time, training will stop. Pay with Cash App or Venmo at @pyrolazuli, Zelle at (323) 774-8524, or cash.

What if I need to cancel?

There’s no charge with at least 24 hours’ notice. Cancellations with less notice and no-shows incur a $22 fee, with discretion to waive it for emergencies.

What happens in bad weather?

If rain or unsafe court conditions prevent training, we’ll reschedule at no charge.

What if Isaiah needs to cancel?

There’s no charge if Isaiah cancels, and the session can be rescheduled.
